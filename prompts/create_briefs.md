# Create three evidence-grounded briefs

Produce exactly three connected but distinct briefs with piece IDs `01`, `02`, and `03`. Use only accepted evidence and the exact stored evidence versions supplied. Each central argument must draw on at least three independent publishers and include at least one inference that connects multiple sources. Every claim needs one or more locators: an exact quote for written text or an exact segment ID for audio/video. Do not use a title or homepage as a locator.

Distinguish `reported_fact`, `source_claim`, and `inference`. Explain the cross-source reasoning in `synthesis`, disclose commercial incentives or conflicts when relevant, and include a meaningful limitation or counterargument. Do not invent disagreement or support. If the evidence cannot meet these rules, fail rather than filling gaps.
