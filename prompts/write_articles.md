# Write the articles

Turn each reviewed brief into an English article for AI and operations-research PhD students. Target 600–900 words excluding source notes. Preserve the brief's argument, uncertainty, and claim IDs. Mark each substantive paragraph with its supporting claim ID in an unobtrusive HTML comment such as `<!-- claim:01-c1 -->`. Explain whether statements are reported facts, source claims, or the publication's inference. Include a limitation or counterargument and practical implications without turning the piece into a generic checklist.

End with reader-facing source notes containing titles, direct URLs, and timestamps for media. Never invent a citation, URL, quote, or conclusion.
