# Write the video scripts

Turn each brief into a 3–5 minute English narration for Google Vids. Use the same claims and evidence as its article. Include concise visual directions, spoken attribution, and claim markers such as `[claim:01-c1]`. Preserve qualifications and do not simplify a source claim into a fact. End with production notes and a source slate containing direct URLs plus media timestamps.

Do not invent visuals that imply unavailable data, and do not invent citations or conclusions.
